THE REASON WE'RE HERE

Made for the
moment it clicks.

My Little Project started with a simple thought: what if we made more time to make things together?

We're a UK, dad-founded project brand working on hands-on kits that invite curious kids and grown-ups to try something new side by side. Our early ideas bring electronics, robotics and discovery into an approachable build.

We want the steps to feel achievable, the learning to happen naturally and the finished project to be something you're excited to show someone else. Solder-free builds and affordable core kits are the direction we're designing for as we test our first prototypes.
