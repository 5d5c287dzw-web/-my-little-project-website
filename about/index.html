// OUR STORY

Little projects.
Big memories._

Hi! I'm a dad building something I hope other families will love: hands-on projects that turn curiosity into something you can actually make.

My Little Project started with a simple idea. Time spent making things together can be just as exciting as the finished result. A box of parts, a bit of trial and error, and that moment when something you've built finally comes to life — that's the magic we're chasing.

We're not just making gadgets. We're creating reasons to put the screens down for a while, get hands-on and discover how things work — together.

Our first ideas bring electronics, coding and robotics into approachable projects, from a little aircraft radar display to a remote-controlled buggy. They're still on the workbench: we're prototyping, testing and learning as we go.

I want the instructions to be clear, the builds to feel achievable and the experience to be something children and grown-ups can enjoy side by side. Not everything has to work first time. Figuring it out is part of the fun.

We're starting small, sharing the journey and taking the time to get the details right before the kits go on sale.
