FROM EVERYDAY QUESTION TO REAL-WORLD DISCOVERY

The sky becomes your next project.

A plane goes over. Someone asks where it came from. Instead of guessing, you build a way to investigate together.

The concept combines an ADS-B receiver, a controller and a round display. You connect the pieces, see how a broadcast becomes information, then use that little screen as a reason to keep looking up. We are still testing the electronics and the build guide.

STRAIGHT FROM THE WORKBENCH

What’s decided.
What’s still being tested.

We want the first kit to be worth your time together. These are development plans, not final specifications or a promise that the project is ready to buy.

Current project note

A little window into a very big sky. We’re prototyping a hands-on aircraft tracking build that brings real-world signals, a display and a good question together.

Build approach
Solder-free assembly is the goal; the prototype and instructions are being tested.
Price
The core kit is targeted under £40. No sale price is set.
Performance
ADS-B reception depends on location, aerial and final design.
Availability
Parts list, safety information, age guidance and release date are not final.
BEFORE YOU ASK

Good questions
to ask now.

Will it show aircraft where I live?

We cannot promise coverage yet. Reception will depend on your location and the final receiver and aerial design.

Will I need to solder?

A solder-free build is the goal. We will confirm the assembly method after prototype testing.

What will be in the box?

The receiver, screen, enclosure and instructions are still being validated. We will publish the exact contents before taking orders.
