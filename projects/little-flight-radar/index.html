Radar_

Build a tiny radar-style display and discover the aircraft flying overhead.

A hands-on electronics project for curious minds. Explore screens, microcontrollers and the fascinating world of real-time flight information — one little build at a time.

What will you discover?_

01 // BUILD

Make it yours

Bring together a microcontroller, round display and enclosure to create a little desk gadget.

Understand the tech

// PLANNED KIT

What's on the workbench?_

Our current prototype explores these parts:

  • ESP32-based microcontroller
  • Compact round colour display
  • Rotary control for interacting with the display
  • Custom-designed enclosure
  • Beginner-friendly build guidance

We're still testing component choices, assembly and software before confirming the final kit contents.

// BEFORE LAUNCH

Made to be built together_

We're aiming for an approachable, hands-on experience that makes the technology understandable and fun.

Prototype in development.

Price, age suitability, build time, included parts, power requirements and availability have not yet been finalised. The flight-data source and its commercial permissions are also under review.
