LP-041 / ROBOTICS & CODING

Little AI Rover.

Build a rover. Teach it what to do.

A robot project with room to grow. We're exploring a solder-free rover that starts with building and coding, then adds sensors and a carefully tested AI vision option.

PROJECT CONCEPT / LP-041
THE IDEA

Build it. Make it yours.

Every detail is being refined around the experience of building and discovering together.

01

Build it together

Explore a shared rover platform designed around approachable assembly and clear, step-by-step support.

02

Code what happens next

Try driving and sensor challenges. Line following and obstacle avoidance are planned coding activities, not artificial intelligence.

03

Explore a vision idea

A higher edition could use a family phone, tablet or computer to recognise selected objects and send a command to the rover. This needs a working prototype before it can be promised.

THREE WAYS TO BUILD

Start small.
Add more when ready.

Early price targets only. No edition is available to order; kit contents and costs have not been validated.

PROTOTYPE IDEA / 01

Build & Drive

£29.99–£39.99 target

A solder-free rover for driving and beginner coding. The controller and all required parts must be costed as part of the kit.

Not for sale · awaiting prototype and BOM
PROTOTYPE IDEA / 02

Smart Explorer

£49.99–£69.99 target

An expanded sensor edition with guided line-following and obstacle-avoidance challenges. These are programmed behaviours, not AI.

Not for sale · awaiting prototype and BOM
PROTOTYPE IDEA / 03

AI Vision Rover

£89.99–£129.99+ target

A proposed premium edition using a phone, tablet or computer for object recognition. Device compatibility, processing method and privacy need a proof of concept.

Not for sale · awaiting prototype and BOM

Our AI promise: sensor routines like line following are programmed behaviours, not AI. We will only call the premium idea AI after a working object-recognition demo, compatible device list and privacy approach are confirmed.

HONESTLY, IT'S STILL ON THE BENCH

What we're
working through.

This is an early product concept. We have not selected the controller, confirmed the bill of materials, built the vision feature or tested privacy, safety, durability and the family guide. Prices below are planning ranges only, not confirmed retail prices.

Who is it for?

A child and grown-up who want to build a small robot, learn how its code works and add new challenges over time.
