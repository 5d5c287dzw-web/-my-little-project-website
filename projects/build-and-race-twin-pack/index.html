// PLANNED KIT

What's on the workbench?_

Our current prototype explores these parts:

  • Two planned ESP32-based buggy builds
  • Two sets of motors, wheels and chassis components
  • Motor-driver electronics for both buggies
  • Two handheld wireless joystick controllers
  • Build instructions and learning activities

We're developing a solder-free assembly approach where practical. Final components and the power solution are still being tested.

// BEFORE LAUNCH

Two builds. Twice the fun._

The goal is a shared build-and-race experience for families and friends, with clear steps and room to experiment.

Prototype in development.

Price, age suitability, build time, exact twin-pack contents, battery requirements and availability are not final. Wireless performance and product safety testing are part of development.
