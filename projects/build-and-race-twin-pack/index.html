A BIGGER BUILD FOR TWO

The race starts at the workbench.

There is something different about building the same thing alongside someone else. You compare approaches, help each other through the tricky bits and finally line up what you made.

The Twin Pack is a concept for two buggy builds and two control options. Its design, contents and price depend on the Little RC Buggy prototype, so this remains an optional larger kit idea rather than a product ready for orders.

STRAIGHT FROM THE WORKBENCH

What’s decided.
What’s still being tested.

We want the first kit to be worth your time together. These are development plans, not final specifications or a promise that the project is ready to buy.

Current project note

Double the building, then line them up for a race. This planned bundle brings two Little RC Buggy builds together for families who want to make and play as a team.

Bundle
Two buggies and two controllers are a proposed configuration, subject to testing.
Price
This is an optional larger kit; price is not confirmed and may exceed £40.
Dependency
The single Little RC Buggy must be validated before this bundle can be finalised.
Availability
Contents, safety information, age guidance and release date are not final.
BEFORE YOU ASK

Good questions
to ask now.

Are there two controllers in the pack?

That is the concept, but the controller design and final bundle contents are not confirmed.

Is this one of the under-£40 kits?

No price is set. We expect this optional larger bundle to sit above the core kit price target.

When can I order?

The single buggy prototype and then the bundle need validation first. We will announce timing when it is reliable.
