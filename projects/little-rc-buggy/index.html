Buggy_

Build your own little robot car. Take control. See where your imagination drives you.

// PLANNED KIT

What's on the workbench?_

Our current prototype explores these parts:

  • ESP32-based buggy controller
  • Compact motors, wheels and chassis
  • Motor-driver electronics
  • Handheld wireless joystick controller
  • Build instructions and learning activities

We're developing a solder-free assembly approach where practical. Final components and the power solution are still being tested.

// BEFORE LAUNCH

Small buggy. Big possibilities._

The goal is a kit that's as rewarding to assemble as it is to drive, with clear steps and room to experiment.

Prototype in development.

Price, age suitability, build time, included parts, battery requirements and availability are not final. Wireless performance and product safety testing are part of development.
