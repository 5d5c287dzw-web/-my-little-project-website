Before you begin

Stage overview

  1. Identify the front and rear of the chassis and the intended wheel positions.
  2. Review how wheel supports, axles and motor mounts are intended to align.
  3. Before final assembly, check that moving parts have adequate clearance according to the verified design.

Questions to resolve

What if a wheel rubs?

Do not force it. Compare alignment and clearances with the approved design once available.

What if a fastener does not fit?

Stop and check its specification; forcing it may damage a printed or threaded part.
