Before you begin

Stage overview

  1. Identify which components are motors, motor drivers, control boards and power sources.
  2. Read the rated voltages, polarity markings and current requirements on approved datasheets.
  3. Wait for a verified wiring diagram and electrical safety review before connecting power.
